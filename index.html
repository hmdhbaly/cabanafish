Company story

We connect Mauritania's Atlantic marine resources with importers, distributors and industrial buyers who require consistent sourcing, export documentation and logistics coordination.

02

Mission

Supply premium fresh and frozen marine products with disciplined product selection, responsive communication and buyer-specific packing requirements.

03

Vision

Become a long-term Mauritanian seafood partner for international buyers across Europe, Asia, the Middle East, Africa and North America.
